<a href="https://www.instagram.com/doomsday.ca/">Doomsday</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/forcedtosufferhc/">Forced to Suffer</a>, <a href="https://www.instagram.com/adjudicatorband/">Adjudicator</a>, and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/m1garandhc/">M1 Garand</a> will be playing a benefit this Saturday at <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/yMzwfJ2Q5Supz3cq9">Drakes Brewery</a> to help out all the families affected by the recent cuts to food aid. All proceeds are going to be donated to the <a href="https://www.instagram.com/accfb/">@accfb</a>. Monetary donations to the food bank go the furthest but shelf-stable foods sitting in your cupboards such as canned meals, cereal, peanut butter, protein bars, etc. are also appreciated.
